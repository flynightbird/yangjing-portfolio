# Portfolio Chinese Copy Editorial Design

**Date:** July 27, 2026

**Scope:** Rewrite directly visible Chinese titles and body copy across the public portfolio. Preserve page structure, component logic, visual design, navigation, controls, accessibility labels, errors, empty states, English pages, and internal drafts.

## Intent

The portfolio should present Yang Jing as a senior product designer for AI and complex systems. The copy should feel restrained, warm, professional, editorial, and natural. It should help hiring decision-makers quickly understand product context, design judgment, role, delivery, and confirmed outcomes without marketing language or inflated claims.

The selected direction is **restrained editorial narrative**:

- homepage and About carry most of the warmth and personality;
- case studies prioritize clarity, decisions, constraints, and confirmed results;
- reflections contain method summaries only;
- short paragraphs and precise verbs replace slogans and process narration.

## 1. Evidence And Gaps

Evidence classification is an internal editing tool and must not become visible page copy.

### Confirmed public claims

- Alibaba App redesign: `MAU +45%`.
- Website upgrade: retention `+35%`.
- Existing Xuelang experiment metrics confirmed by the designer.
- Existing responsibilities, deliverables, and launch states may remain when established in project materials or confirmed by the designer.

### Editing rules

- Write only confirmable responsibilities, deliverables, launch states, experiment data, and observable product changes.
- Never turn a design goal, expected effect, or product intention into a measured outcome.
- When result evidence is absent, omit the unsupported result content instead of displaying a disclaimer, evidence boundary, empty result statement, or speculative benefit.
- Remove all visible disclaimers and all visible language about evidence boundaries.
- Preserve individual and team contribution boundaries. Do not imply sole ownership of team output.
- Use `独立负责产品设计` as the standard public role label where the existing copy refers to the designer as the sole or primary product designer.

## 2. Positioning

### Portfolio positioning

Yang Jing is a product designer (UI/UX) focused on AI and complex systems, turning research, product constraints, and emerging technology into understandable workflows and runnable experiences.

### Identity rules

- Keep `Yang Jing` in the brand and navigation.
- Use `杨静` in Chinese prose and the About narrative.
- Use the Chinese identity headline `产品设计师（UI/UX），专注 AI 与复杂系统`.
- Keep `AI-native Builder` and `Vibe Coding` in English.
- Present building and coding as accelerators to product design judgment, not as equal primary identities.
- Retain the current Chinese-English mix and standardize English spelling and capitalization.
- Keep professional terms in English without forced Chinese explanations.

## 3. Chapter Architecture

Existing page and chapter structures remain unchanged. Editing changes emphasis inside those structures.

### Homepage

- Establish identity, focus, and representative work immediately.
- Keep project-card descriptions to one or two sentences.
- First state what the project is, then the consequential problem or design focus.
- Do not repeat information already present in titles, tags, or metadata.
- Use a warm, direct invitation instead of a marketing claim.

### About

- Build one concise career throughline: user research -> scaled consumer products -> complex systems -> AI products -> runnable prototypes.
- Explain how capability developed instead of listing jobs chronologically without a point of view.
- De-emphasize design leadership as a primary positioning signal.
- Keep the tone personal enough to reveal motivation, but restrained and professional.

### Case studies

- Preserve current chapters and unique project facts.
- Make the opening quickly establish product, problem, role, and launch state.
- Organize body copy around context, constraint, judgment, design action, and confirmed result.
- Describe features through the reason for the design decision, not as a capability inventory.
- Preserve key decisions and role boundaries when shortening.
- End reflections with reusable method summaries only. Do not add future opportunities, self-evaluation, or `what I would do next` content.

### Build pages

- Preserve their experimental and technical character.
- Explain which product judgment the experiment tested.
- Avoid turning the page into a tool list or implementation log.
- Do not force established English technical language into Chinese.

### Footer

- Close with a warm, direct invitation to contact.
- Avoid campaign language, grand claims, and feature explanations.

## 4. Visual Direction

No visual or layout changes are included. Copy must fit the existing typography and responsive containers.

Editorial rhythm comes from language:

- descriptive headings rather than judgment-led slogans;
- one idea per paragraph;
- shorter sentences and concrete verbs;
- low modifier density;
- natural Chinese syntax rather than sentence-by-sentence translation from English.

## 5. Visual Asset Plan

No asset additions, removals, reordering, captions, or visual annotations are in scope. Existing media remains untouched. Copy edits must not require a new visual asset to make a claim credible.

## 6. Screen-Level Copy Treatment

When existing copy explains a product screen, retain the useful parts of the CPDI logic without exposing framework labels:

- orient the reader to where the user is;
- state the relevant user or system tension;
- explain the consequential design decision and tradeoff;
- state a confirmed result only when evidence exists;
- otherwise stop after the observable change in the interface or workflow.

Screen descriptions that merely list visible controls should be compressed or removed.

## 7. Three-Minute Narrative

The portfolio scan should communicate this sequence:

1. Yang Jing is a product designer (UI/UX) focused on AI and complex systems.
2. Her experience connects user research, scaled consumer products, complex workflows, and AI prototyping.
3. Representative cases demonstrate how she frames problems, makes design decisions, and brings work into a delivered or testable state.
4. Confirmed results support the story where available; unsupported impact is omitted.
5. The portfolio closes with a direct invitation to talk.

## 8. Ten-Minute Deep Dive

Each case study should support a deeper read without adding new page logic:

- product and user context;
- the central problem and relevant constraints;
- the designer's exact role;
- the pivotal insight or product judgment;
- consequential interaction or system decisions;
- collaboration and delivery state;
- confirmed experiment or product results when available;
- a concise method reflection.

The deep read must remain consistent with the faster homepage and case-opening summaries.

## 9. Interview Readiness

Public copy should give Yang Jing a credible foundation for discussing:

- what she independently owned and what the team contributed;
- why a design decision was made;
- which constraints shaped the solution;
- how AI behavior and human control were divided;
- what shipped or was tested;
- which results are confirmed;
- what method transferred to later work.

Interview questions and answer scripts are not public copy and are outside this implementation scope.

## 10. Hiring-Manager Evaluation And Priorities

The final editorial pass is evaluated in this order:

1. **Positioning clarity:** identity and AI/complex-systems focus are understandable in one scan.
2. **Decision quality:** cases explain why key choices were made.
3. **Role clarity:** individual responsibility and team contribution are not blurred.
4. **Complexity to clarity:** dense product material reads as a coherent argument rather than documentation.
5. **Credibility:** goals and intentions are not presented as outcomes.
6. **Human tone:** homepage, About, and reflections feel natural and specific without sentimentality.
7. **Editorial consistency:** terminology, capitalization, role labels, numbers, and paragraph rhythm are consistent across pages.

## Language Rules

### Prefer

- concrete subjects and active verbs;
- specific product nouns;
- direct descriptions of judgment and action;
- short paragraphs with one point each;
- restrained warmth in homepage, About, reflection, and footer copy.

### Remove Or Rewrite

Remove generic phrases when they do not carry a specific project meaning, including:

- `赋能`
- `打造`
- `沉淀`
- `闭环`
- `抓手`
- `全链路`
- `体验升级`
- `能力建设`
- `价值落地`

Do not overcorrect into slang or deliberately casual language. Keep a conventional term when it is the clearest accurate expression.

## Public Scope

Rewrite only directly visible public Chinese titles and body copy in:

- homepage content;
- About content;
- published Chinese case studies;
- published Chinese Build pages;
- directly visible localized text embedded in their components;
- footer invitation and contact copy.

Do not change:

- buttons and navigation;
- `alt` text and `aria-label` values;
- errors and empty states;
- internal drafts and documentation;
- English content;
- page logic, information architecture, components, styles, or media.

## Implementation And Verification

1. Inventory public Chinese strings and classify each as keep, shorten, rewrite, or remove.
2. Normalize identity, role wording, terminology, capitalization, metrics, and launch-state language.
3. Edit homepage and About to establish the shared voice.
4. Edit each published case and Build page without changing its structure.
5. Remove visible disclaimers, evidence-boundary text, unsupported outcomes, and redundant passages.
6. Search for banned filler phrases, inconsistent role labels, unsupported result language, and hard-translated Chinese.
7. Run MDX checks, type checks, and the production build.
8. Review the Chinese homepage, About page, and every published Chinese case route at desktop and mobile widths for overflow or broken hierarchy caused by changed copy.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The portfolio consistently positions Yang Jing as `产品设计师（UI/UX），专注 AI 与复杂系统`.
- Public copy contains no visible disclaimers or evidence-boundary explanations.
- Unconfirmed outcomes are omitted rather than softened into speculative claims.
- Confirmed metrics remain accurate and traceable to their existing source content.
- Role wording is consistent and contribution boundaries remain credible.
- Chinese reads as independently edited prose, not literal translation.
- Existing routes, structure, controls, visual design, and media remain unchanged.
- The project compiles successfully and edited pages remain readable at supported responsive widths.
